Built for UK gallons.

Most fuel trackers use US gallons by default. Fuelled UK calculates MPG using imperial gallons, which is the figure UK drivers actually expect.

Key features

  • Multi-vehicle support with colour-coded charts
  • Accurate UK MPG calculations using imperial gallons
  • Log fill-ups with litres, mileage, and total cost in pounds
  • Complete fill-up history with edit and delete support
  • MPG and cost-per-litre charts
  • MOT, tax, and insurance renewal reminders
  • Automatic iCloud sync across your devices
  • No account required

See the trend.

Fuelled UK builds a clearer picture over time: how each vehicle performs, whether your MPG is improving, and how pump prices affect your running costs.

Never miss a renewal.

Store the MOT, tax, and insurance expiry dates for each vehicle and turn on optional reminders. Fuelled UK sends local notifications ahead of every deadline — a month, two weeks, a week, and a day before — so nothing creeps up on you. Your dates sync privately across your devices via iCloud.
