Fuelled UK

Fuel & MPG tracker for UK cars

Download on the App Store

Built for UK Drivers, Not US Gallons

Most fuel tracker apps get UK MPG wrong because they use US gallons. Fuelled UK uses imperial gallons (4.546 litres) — the only correct way to calculate miles per gallon in the UK.

Know Your Real Running Costs

Log each fill-up and Fuelled UK builds a full picture of your fuel economy and costs over time. See your MPG per fill, your running average, and what you're paying per litre — all laid out in clean charts that make trends easy to spot.

Key Features

  • Multi-vehicle support with colour-coded charts for each vehicle
  • Accurate UK MPG calculations using imperial gallons (4.546 litres)
  • Log fill-ups with litres, mileage, and total cost (£)
  • Complete fill-up history with edit and delete support
  • MPG chart with per-fill economy and running average trend line
  • Cost per litre chart with separate lines for each vehicle
  • Automatic iCloud sync across all your devices
  • No account required — data lives in your own iCloud

Track Multiple Vehicles

Got more than one car? No problem. Fuelled UK lets you add as many vehicles as you need, each with its own colour-coded history and charts. Switch between them instantly from the home screen or filter charts by vehicle to compare economy side by side.

See the Trend, Not Just the Number

A single MPG figure doesn't tell you much. The running average trend line on the MPG chart shows you whether your fuel economy is improving or declining over time — far more useful than any one-off reading. The cost per litre chart helps you track how pump prices are affecting your wallet fill by fill.

Private by Default

Everything is stored in your private iCloud account. No external servers, no accounts, no data collection. Your driving habits and costs are yours alone. The App works fully offline too — iCloud sync happens in the background when you're connected.

Try It Free

Fuelled UK includes a full 7-day free trial so you can log a few fill-ups and see it in action before committing. After the trial, a single one-time purchase of £4.99 unlocks lifetime access — no subscriptions, no recurring charges.
