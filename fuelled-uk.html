Built for UK gallons.

Most fuel trackers use US gallons by default. Fuelled UK uses imperial gallons, so you get the MPG figure UK drivers actually expect.

Key features

  • Track as many vehicles as you like, each with its own fuel type and colour
  • Accurate UK MPG calculations using imperial gallons
  • Log fill-ups with litres, mileage, and total cost in pounds; MPG and cost per litre are calculated automatically
  • Complete fill-up history with edit and delete support
  • Cost per Mile summary on the home screen: latest pence per mile, trend, legs, total spent, miles covered and average MPG for the selected vehicle
  • MPG and cost-per-litre charts, with each vehicle shown as its own line
  • MOT, tax, and insurance renewal reminders
  • Colour-coded renewal status in Manage Vehicles: green, amber, or expired
  • Automatic iCloud sync across your devices; works offline and syncs when a connection is available
  • No account required

See the trend.

The longer you use Fuelled UK, the clearer the picture gets: how each vehicle is doing, whether your MPG is improving, and how pump prices are hitting your running costs. The Cost per Mile summary at the top of the home screen shows the latest pence per mile, the trend, legs, total spent, miles covered and average MPG for the selected vehicle.

Never miss a renewal.

Add the MOT, tax and insurance expiry dates for each vehicle and turn on reminders if you want them. Fuelled UK sends a local notification a month, two weeks, a week and a day before each deadline, so nothing creeps up on you. In Manage Vehicles, each vehicle has a colour-coded status (green, amber or expired), so you can see where things stand at a glance. Your dates sync privately across your devices via iCloud.
