Read this before you join the TestFlight.

Eve R is a companion app. She only works if Eve is installed and running on your Mac — her memory lives there, and the phone reaches it over your own private Tailscale network. If you don't have the Mac app set up, Eve R cannot work for you, so please get Eve for Mac first and don't take up a tester slot.

Mandatory requirements

What you'll need before Eve R works.

All four of these are required. Without them, the app will not work:

  • Eve for Mac — installed, set up, and running on your Mac. Eve R is her companion and can't function alone.
  • An xAI account — either an xAI subscription you can sign in with, or an xAI API key. This powers Eve's voice and conversation.
  • A DeepSeek API key — this powers Eve's long-term memory, distilling and organising everything she remembers about you.
  • Tailscale on both devices — 100% free on Mac and iPhone. This is the private network your phone uses to reach Eve's memory on your Mac, and it's mandatory.

The xAI and DeepSeek credentials are set up once in Eve on your Mac — your paired phone picks them up from there.

How it works

Your voice travels light. Her memory stays home.

When you talk to Eve R, your voice connects directly from your iPhone to xAI's realtime voice service — quick and natural, with the same flowing conversation you get on the Mac.

Everything Eve remembers, though, never leaves your Mac. The phone reaches her memory over your own Tailscale network, so your memories stay on hardware you own — and you can browse her whole memory map right from your phone.

Set up in seconds

Pair once, then just talk.

Open Eve on your Mac, show the pairing code, and scan it with Eve R on your iPhone. That's the whole setup — from then on she reconnects automatically whenever both are online.

One voice everywhere: choose Eve's voice on either device and the other picks it up within half a minute.

Keeping her reachable: Eve's memory lives on your Mac, so the Mac needs to stay awake for the phone to reach it. Turn on Keep Mac alive — it's at the bottom of the Settings screen on the phone, so scroll down — and make sure your MacBook itself is configured to stay awake too.

Key features

  • The same Eve you talk to on your Mac, now on your iPhone
  • Natural voice conversation straight from the phone to xAI
  • Her long-term memory stays on your Mac — recalled mid-conversation wherever you are
  • Browse her memory from your phone
  • Pairing by QR code — no accounts, no passwords to type
  • Connects over your private Tailscale network, not the public internet
  • One voice across iPhone and Mac, kept in sync automatically
  • Free — runs on your own xAI and DeepSeek accounts
