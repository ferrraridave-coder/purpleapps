About Me – My Story

I operate a loading shovel in a quarry. I start at 6:30 AM, finish at 4:45 PM, and I’ve been doing it for over fifteen years. By night, early mornings, and weekends, I build iOS apps.

I’m 56 years old. No computer science degree. No bootcamp. Just a MacBook, a lot of stubbornness, and a refusal to quit.

This is how it happened.

The Problem That Started It All

I’ve always been obsessed with technology. Not casually interested — obsessed. I need to understand how things work, how they break, and how they can be improved.

When AI tools started appearing, I didn’t fear them. I embraced them. But the apps I tried were never quite right. Limited features, clunky interfaces, decisions made for users instead of empowering them.

Eventually I reached the point where frustration turned into a question: why doesn’t the app I want exist?

And then the obvious follow-up: why don’t I just build it myself?

The First Week

I bought a MacBook. Then reality hit.

Xcode. GitHub. App Store Connect. Certificates. Provisioning profiles. None of it made sense. All of it was intimidating.

I didn’t follow tutorials or courses. I learned by building, breaking things, fixing them, and repeating that cycle relentlessly.

I’d already committed money and time, which meant quitting was no longer an option. So I kept going.

The Reality of the Grind

This isn’t a motivational montage. This is the actual routine:

  • 5:00 AM – Code before work
  • 6:30 AM – 4:45 PM – Operate heavy machinery
  • Evenings – Code until late
  • Weekends – Code almost nonstop

That’s on top of running infrastructure, paying for APIs, maintaining servers, and handling App Store submissions.

Why is it sustainable? Because I enjoy it. Deeply.

Using AI (Properly)

AI has been a huge part of the journey. I use it for learning, debugging, architecture decisions, and problem-solving.

But AI isn’t magic. It doesn’t experience friction. It doesn’t feel when something is awkward to use.

The best results come when human judgment and AI capability work together. AI accelerates. Humans decide.

The Apps

Chat LLM

My first app. Privacy-focused AI chat with optional local AI, cloud models, voice chat, and advanced control. It took hundreds of hours and taught me how Apple really works.

VisuAIly

Built because I wanted image and video generation done properly. Not just functional — beautiful. Polished. Intentional.

Microscope AI

Built in a single weekend out of curiosity. It exists because sometimes the best ideas appear when you stop forcing them.

Flash Image & LLM Pro

These came from experience. Faster development. Clearer decisions. Better instincts. The learning curve had flattened.

What I’ve Learned

I’ve learned that age is irrelevant. That creativity can appear later in life. And that learning never actually stops.

Building apps isn’t just writing code. It’s design, judgement, patience, and taste.

I don’t chase trends. I fix problems. And I care deeply about how things feel to use.

Why I Do This

This is a one-person operation. I’m not building a company. I’m building things I’m proud of.

Things that work properly. Things that respect users. Things I can pass down.

Something More Personal

Alongside my technical work, I also write a small children’s book series inspired by my granddaughter.

It’s a completely different kind of creativity — slower, warmer, and personal — and something I care deeply about.
