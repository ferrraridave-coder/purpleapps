Bars show the average level over each day; tap or click any day for that day's high/low and how much is still active from earlier jabs. The model assumes each jab absorbs slowly from the injection site (peaking 12–72 hours post-injection) then decays with an approximately 6-day half-life, based on published clinical trial data.
