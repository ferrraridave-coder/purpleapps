Peptide Levels Calculator

Estimated mg active in the body per day. Pick the peptides you're running, set your dose and jab spacing, tap any day for the detail. Model: first-order absorption (slow depot release, peaks ~8–72h post-jab) + exponential elimination, from published trial half-life data.

All peptides are switched off — pick one above to see its levels.
Bars show the average level over each day — one lane per active peptide, each with its own figure, growing into place from the week you start it. Tap or click any day for that day's breakdown. The model assumes each jab absorbs slowly from the injection site (peaking 8–72 hours post-injection) then decays exponentially — retatrutide at an approximately 6-day half-life, cagrilintide ~7 days, tirzepatide ~5 days, semaglutide ~7 days, based on published clinical trial data.
