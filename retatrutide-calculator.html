Bars show the average level over each day. When you're stacking, they stack — gold for retatrutide, teal for cagrilintide, with the combined total at the end. Tap or click any day for that day's breakdown. The model assumes each jab absorbs slowly from the injection site (peaking 12–72 hours post-injection) then decays exponentially — retatrutide at an approximately 6-day half-life, cagrilintide at approximately 7 days, based on published clinical trial data.
