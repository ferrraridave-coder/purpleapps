Chat LLM

AI chat for people who want choice: on-device AI, fast free cloud chat, 300+ models via OpenRouter, real-time voice chat with Gemini or Grok, your own computer’s models through LM Studio, and a document Reader that speaks PDFs, EPUBs, text, and webpages in premium voices.

Flexible by design

Use local AI for private offline chats, try free cloud chat without an account, or connect your own provider keys when you want more control over models and capabilities.

Voice chat with Gemini & Grok.

Have natural, real-time spoken conversations with AI through two backends. Google Gemini Live brings a choice of voices, built-in Google Search for live information, and the ability to share photos mid-conversation. xAI Grok adds a second real-time backend with a large, automatically-updated library of voices spanning many languages and accents.

Each backend has its own independent system prompts, so you can shape personality, accent, and behaviour for each one. Bring your own Gemini and xAI keys.

Run your own models with LM Studio.

Run any open model on your computer in LM Studio and use it from Chat LLM anywhere over a private Tailscale connection. Browse, load, and unload your computer’s models right from the app, with the same chat experience as every other provider.

No per-message credits, no rate limits, and no content restrictions — the only cost is your own electricity and broadband, and your chat stays on your own hardware.

Reader — listen to anything.

Reader turns Chat LLM into an audiobook player for your own content. Import a PDF, EPUB, or TXT file, paste any text, or share a webpage or document straight into the app, and Reader speaks it paragraph by paragraph. Tap any paragraph to jump to it, and your position is saved automatically.

Choose your voice per document from four text-to-speech providers — OpenRouter Kokoro for pennies, MiniMax for high quality at great value, ElevenLabs for expressive narration with your own cloned voices, or Fish Audio for a vast library of community and custom voices in countless accents. Bring your own key for the provider you pick; your account voices sync automatically and you can search and favourite them.

Want to keep a document for good? Export it to a single audio file saved right on your device. From then on it plays straight from that file — offline, with no further voice credits used — and you can share the audio out to other apps. Exported files also show up in the Files app under Chat LLM → Reader.

Key features

  • Optional on-device AI for offline chat with no internet connection required
  • Free cloud chat available without creating an account
  • 300+ third-party models via OpenRouter (bring your own key)
  • LM Studio — use your own computer’s local models over a private Tailscale connection
  • Real-time voice chat with Google Gemini Live and xAI Grok — Grok offers a large, auto-updating library of voices across many languages and accents (bring your own keys)
  • Reader — spoken playback of PDFs, EPUBs, TXT files, pasted text, and shared webpages, with OpenRouter Kokoro, MiniMax, ElevenLabs, or Fish Audio voices (BYOK)
  • Per-document voice and speed, saved position, and a built-in library
  • Share any document, webpage, or text into Reader to start listening instantly
  • Export a whole Reader document to a single saved audio file — play it back offline without using your voice credits again, share it, or open it from the Files app
  • Web search toggle for any OpenRouter model — live results in text chat (BYOK, charged to your OpenRouter credits)
  • Image and photo uploads on supported vision-capable models
  • Document uploads for PDF, Word, and text files where supported
  • Independent primary and secondary system prompts per provider, for both text and voice
  • Advanced generation controls (temperature, top-p, and more)
  • iCloud sync — your settings, system prompts, conversations, and Reader library sync across your devices and restore after reinstalling
  • Conversation history with model identification

Built for privacy-conscious users.

Chat LLM does not require a user account. Optional API keys are stored securely on your device using iOS Keychain.

Your conversations and Reader library sync through your own private iCloud, tied to your Apple ID — they are never stored on our servers, and we have no access to them.

When you use third-party providers, your prompts, uploads, or voice input are sent to the selected provider to generate a response. Their own privacy and retention policies apply.
