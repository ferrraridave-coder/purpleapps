Built for insurance, peace of mind, and the day something goes wrong — without putting your home on someone else’s server.

The Vault

Backed up. Export-ready. Off-device when you say so.

The Vault is where backup health and proof live. When unlock is active, inventory syncs to your private iCloud. Full-resolution photos stay on the device; thumbnails travel so lists still look right on another iPhone or iPad signed into the same Apple ID.

Export a timestamped proof-of-ownership PDF, a spreadsheet-friendly CSV, or AirDrop the vault to a partner — only when you choose to share.

Walkthrough

Walk a room. Narrate what you see.

Point the walkthrough at a room, tap the mic, and speak naturally. EmberSafe structures name, category, value, and serial on your device with local parsing — nothing is sent to a cloud AI for that step.

You always review and edit the fields before saving. Free tier is enough to start; lifetime unlock removes the room and item caps.

Rooms and items

Your home, organised by room.

See total insured value at a glance, open a room for its list, and drill into any item for photo, purchase details, serial, and warranty cover. Warranty heartbeats nudge you before cover lapses.

Attach photos and receipts as you go. Swipe to delete when something moves on.

Item detail

Proof on every possession that matters.

Each item can carry a photo, category, value, purchase date, serial number, warranty end date, and notes. That is the record you want when you claim, sell, or simply need to remember what you own.
