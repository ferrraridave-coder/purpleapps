Completely Free, No Strings Attached

Microscope AI is 100% free with absolutely no ads. I built this as a tool for the AI community.

Explore the AI Model Landscape

Microscope AI gives you a deeper way to understand AI models, explore capabilities, pricing, context windows, and more, all beautifully organized and easy to navigate.

Key Features

  • AI-powered model analysis using secure Groq processing
  • Detailed side-by-side model comparisons (personality, traits, quirks, biases, context windows, and capabilities)
  • Cost calculator – estimate input/output costs in tokens, characters, or words
  • Powerful sorting and filtering (by name, price, size, context windows, and more)
  • Save your favorite models locally with iCloud sync
  • Beautiful dark theme with smooth animations
  • No accounts required – your data stays on your device

Perfect For

Whether you're an AI researcher, enthusiast exploring model behavior, developer comparing models, or anyone wanting deeper insight into how AI models differ, Microscope AI helps you make sense of today's rapidly evolving model landscape.

Privacy-First, Always Free

No data collection, no tracking, no ads—ever. Analysis text is processed securely through my Vercel backend using industry-standard encryption. Your saved profiles stay on your device and sync privately via iCloud.
