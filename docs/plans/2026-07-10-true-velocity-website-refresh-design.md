# True Velocity website refresh

## Goal

Update the Purple Apps website to present True Velocity's three interchangeable analogue dials, three leather-grain backgrounds, swipe controls, trip recording, pause/resume, and Apple Maps route view accurately.

## Approved design

- Replace the old True Velocity screenshot in the homepage hero with the new Black leather and Frost dial image.
- Keep the unchanged Trips screenshot on the homepage True Velocity card.
- Show Black/Frost, Tan/Classic, and Oxblood/Cerise together in the True Velocity detail-page hero.
- Explain that the three dials and three backgrounds can be mixed independently for nine combinations.
- Explain both selection methods: the Settings pickers and horizontal swipes on the dial/readout or background.
- Move the unchanged Trips screenshot into a dedicated trip-recording section alongside pause/resume, saved trip cards, and Apple Maps route copy.
- Correct the support FAQ so its pause/stop and direct-map instructions match the current app.
- Leave privacy and terms pages unchanged.
- Use new, web-optimised image filenames to avoid stale CDN caches.
