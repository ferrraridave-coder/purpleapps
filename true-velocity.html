Real Speed. Real Feel.

True Velocity uses GPS to measure your actual speed and displays it on a physically-tuned analogue dial — built to feel like a real vehicle speedometer, not a digital number jumping around.

More Than Just a Number

Most GPS speedometer apps just throw a raw number at you. True Velocity goes further — the analogue needle responds with natural inertia, accelerating and decelerating the way a real dial would. Pair that with an adaptive digital readout and you get something that genuinely feels right.

Key Features

  • Smooth analogue dial with physically-tuned needle — mimics real vehicle speedometer feel
  • Real-time GPS speed tracking with adaptive smoothing
  • Clear digital speed display alongside the analogue dial

Engineered Motion Model

Raw GPS speed data can be noisy and jumpy. True Velocity's engineered motion model smooths out those fluctuations so the needle moves the way your gut expects it to — responsive but not jittery.
