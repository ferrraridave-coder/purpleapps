Real speed. Real trips.

True Velocity gives you a clear speedometer and lets you record trips manually when you want journey history — with pause and resume so stops along the way don't wreck your totals.

More than a speed readout.

The analogue needle uses a tuned motion model so it feels closer to a real vehicle speedometer. The large digital readout keeps the current speed obvious at a glance.

When trip recording is enabled, a record button appears on the main screen so you can start and stop journeys manually. Pause mid-trip if you pull in somewhere — then resume when you're back on the road. Saved trips appear as cards for later review.

See where you've been.

Enable Map Visualisation to record your route alongside the trip summary. Tap any saved trip to view it on an interactive Apple Maps view — your full route drawn out, stored privately on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

Key features

  • Smooth analogue dial with physically-tuned needle motion
  • Real-time GPS speed tracking with adaptive smoothing
  • Clear digital speed display alongside the analogue dial
  • Optional trip recorder that can be toggled on or off
  • Manual start, stop, and pause controls for recorded trips
  • Recorded trip cards with duration, distance, average and top speed
  • Map Visualisation — view saved routes on an interactive Apple Maps view
  • Miles per hour and kilometres per hour support
  • No account or sign-in required

Location access that matches the feature.

Live speed display uses GPS while you are using the app. Trip recording may require Always location access so a trip can continue if the app moves into the background, such as during a phone call. Background location is disabled the moment you stop the trip.
