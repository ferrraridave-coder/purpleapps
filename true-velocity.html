Pair any dial with any leather background, then use the physically-tuned speedometer as a clear standalone instrument or record the journeys that matter.

Make it yours

Three dials. Three leathers. Nine combinations.

Classic, Cerise, and Frost dials can each be paired with Black, Oxblood, or Tan leather. The dial and background are independent, so you can build the dashboard combination that suits you rather than being locked into three presets.

Choose both from the segmented pickers in Settings, or change them without leaving the speedometer: swipe left or right across the dial or large digital number to cycle the dial, and swipe across the leather background to cycle the wallpaper. Your choices are saved automatically.

Trip recording

Record the road, not the stops.

Turn on Log Trips and the red record control appears on the speedometer. Tap it to start, tap again to pause or resume, and use the separate square stop control when the journey is finished. Paused time is excluded, while each saved card keeps the duration, distance, average speed, and top speed.

Enable Map Visualisation before recording and tapping a saved trip opens its route directly in an interactive Apple Maps view. Route coordinates stay stored locally on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

Location access that matches the feature.

Live speed display uses GPS while you are using the app. Trip recording may require Always location access so a trip can continue if the app moves into the background, such as during a phone call. Background location is disabled the moment you stop the trip.
