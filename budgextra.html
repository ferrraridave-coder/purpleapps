Budgextra

Always know where your money stands

Download on the App Store

Completely Free. No Strings.

Budgextra is 100% free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. Budgeting help should be available to everyone — especially the people who need it most.

Simple Monthly Budgeting That Actually Works

Budgextra gives you a clear monthly overview of your finances. Track your bills, income, and one-off expenses in one place, mark bills as paid with a single tap, and always know exactly where your money stands. No spreadsheets, no complexity — just the essentials done well.

Key Features

  • Monthly bill tracking with due date management
  • Multi-source income tracking
  • One-time expense recording
  • Single-tap bill payment marking
  • Automatic month-to-month balance carryover
  • Optional overdraft support with customisable limits
  • Visual indicators for negative balance states
  • Manual balance adjustment
  • Optional Face ID security protection
  • Full offline functionality with optional iCloud sync

Built for Real Life

Bills roll over month to month automatically, so you're not rebuilding your budget from scratch every month. Enable overdraft support if you need it, set your own limit, and Budgextra will show you clearly when you're heading into the red. A quick manual adjustment handles anything unexpected.

Your Data Stays Yours

Everything is stored locally on your device. Optional iCloud sync keeps your data backed up and available across devices — but it all lives in your private Apple account, not on anyone else's servers. No account required, no data collected, no analytics. Ever.

Lock It Down with Face ID

If you share your device or just want peace of mind, enable Face ID protection so your financial data stays private. The lock screen activates automatically when you leave the app.
