Budgextra

A monthly budget tracker reimagined as a tactile paper ledger, with handwritten entries, paid stamps, paper menus, and an app-wide parchment finish.

Completely free.

Budgextra has no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. It is designed to help you see where your money stands without adding more friction.

Key features

  • App-wide paper-ledger design with six selectable fonts
  • Monthly bill tracking with due dates
  • Income and one-off expense recording
  • Bill payment marking with clear PAID stamps
  • Automatic month-to-month balance carryover
  • Optional overdraft support
  • Manual balance adjustment
  • Optional Face ID protection
  • Offline functionality with optional iCloud sync
Paper throughout

One ledger, from the main screen to Settings.

The parchment texture carries through Settings, forms, menus, and confirmation dialogs. Six selectable ledger fonts keep the numbers practical while letting the page feel personal.

Built around a real month.

Bills roll over automatically, settled items fade beneath a red PAID stamp, and your current balance remains visible. It keeps the essentials close without turning budgeting into spreadsheet work.
