Copy Tiles: Keyboard

Your most-used text, one tap away

Download on the App Store

Stop Retyping the Same Things

Copy Tiles lets you create a personal library of reusable text snippets — prompts, email addresses, links, canned replies, notes, anything — and insert them from a custom keyboard without ever leaving the app you're in.

A Keyboard That Works for You

Once you've set up your tiles, they live right inside your keyboard. Switch to the Copy Tiles keyboard in any app and tap a tile to paste it instantly. No copying, no switching apps, no retyping. Just tap and done.

Key Features

  • Unlimited tiles and categories — organise your snippets however you like
  • Drag-and-drop reordering for both tiles and categories
  • Adjustable tile sizes — Small (4 per row), Medium (3 per row), or Large (2 per row)
  • Clipboard history accessible directly from the keyboard
  • Full iCloud sync — your tiles are available on every device
  • Real-time layout updates between the app and the keyboard
  • No account required — your data stays in your iCloud

Organised Your Way

Group your tiles into categories that make sense for you — Work, Personal, AI Prompts, Customer Replies, whatever fits. Reorder everything with drag-and-drop, and choose the tile size that suits how you use it. Compact grid for lots of tiles, larger tiles if you prefer easier tapping.

Privacy by Design

Copy Tiles stores everything in your personal iCloud. There are no external servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no data collection. Clipboard history never syncs across devices — it stays local to the device you're on. What you type is yours.

Copy Tiles comes with a full 7-day free trial. After the trial, a single one-time purchase unlocks lifetime access with no subscriptions or recurring charges of any kind.
