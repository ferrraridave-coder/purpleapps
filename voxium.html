Voxium

Turn documents, webpages, and pasted text into an audiobook. Start with a free private voice that runs on your device, then choose a cloud voice when you want one.

Your library. Your place. Your voice.

Import the things you actually want to read, then let Voxium keep your place, handle the pauses, and make the listening experience yours.

Made for listening

Read along, or just press play.

Voxium highlights the sentence being spoken so you can follow every word, skip around when you need to, and return to exactly where you stopped. Set a sleep timer when the book is taking you to bed.

Import PDFs, EPUBs, DOCX files, plain text, webpages, or anything you paste into the app. Your library and reading position are saved automatically.

Your voice, your way

Private by default. Premium when you choose.

Voxium includes a free on-device voice that runs privately on your iPhone or iPad after a one-time download. No account and no API key are needed to get started.

Want something different? Add your own MiniMax, ElevenLabs, or Fish Audio key and choose the voice that suits your book. Fine-tune speed, sentence pauses, paragraph pauses, and auto-play to make narration feel right.

More than playback

Take your listening with you.

Export a document as an audio file for offline listening, open API Keys when you want to connect a premium voice, and clean up shared webpages before narration. Everything is close at hand without getting in the way of the book.

With your own iCloud account, Voxium keeps your library, settings, and progress ready on your other devices too.
