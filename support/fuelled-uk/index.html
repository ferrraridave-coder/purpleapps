Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fuelled UK?

Fuelled UK is a fuel and MPG tracker for UK drivers. You log each fill-up — how many litres you put in, your current mileage, and what you paid — and the app calculates your MPG and cost per litre over time, displayed in charts that make your fuel economy easy to understand at a glance.

Why does Fuelled UK use imperial gallons?

In the UK, miles per gallon is calculated using the imperial gallon, which is 4.546 litres. Many fuel tracker apps incorrectly use the US gallon (3.785 litres), which gives a lower and inaccurate MPG reading for UK drivers. Fuelled UK uses imperial gallons throughout, so your MPG figures match what your car manufacturer quotes and what UK motoring publications use.

How do I log a fill-up?

Tap the + button on the home screen and select your vehicle. Enter the number of litres you put in, your current mileage reading, and the total amount you paid in pounds. Fuelled UK will calculate your MPG and cost per litre automatically and add the fill-up to your history.

How do I add a vehicle?

Tap the vehicle management option from the home screen and use the + button to add a new vehicle. Give it a name and choose a colour. You can add as many vehicles as you need and switch between them from the home screen at any time.

How do I edit or delete a fill-up?

Go to your fill-up history, find the entry you want to change, and swipe left to reveal the edit and delete options. Deletions are permanent and cannot be undone, so double-check before confirming.

What do the charts show?

The MPG chart plots your fuel economy for each individual fill-up, with a dashed running average trend line so you can see whether your economy is improving or declining over time. The cost per litre chart shows what you paid per litre on each fill-up, with separate lines for each vehicle so you can compare across your fleet.

Does Fuelled UK sync across my devices?

Yes. Fuelled UK uses iCloud to automatically sync your fill-up history and vehicles across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID. No account is required — everything lives in your private iCloud.

Does Fuelled UK collect any of my data?

No. Fuelled UK does not collect any personal data. Your fill-up history and vehicle data are stored in your private iCloud account. There are no external servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

Is there a subscription?

No. Fuelled UK offers a full 7-day free trial, after which a single one-time purchase of £4.99 unlocks lifetime access. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges. All payments are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the restore option in the app's settings or on the paywall screen. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID and can be restored at no cost on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

What devices does Fuelled UK support?

Fuelled UK requires an iPhone or iPad running iOS 18.6 or later.
