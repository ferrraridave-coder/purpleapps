Privacy Policy for Fuelled UK

Last updated: April 2026

Fuelled UK ("the App") is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data when you use it on iPhone or iPad.

Support Page Terms of Use Back to Fuelled UK

1. Data You Enter

Fuelled UK stores the data you enter — vehicle details, fill-up records (litres, mileage, and cost), and any edits or deletions you make — locally on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your private Apple iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. We do not have access to your fill-up history or vehicle data at any time.

2. iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is built in and operates automatically when you are signed into iCloud on your device. Your data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us. We do not operate any servers that store or process your fuel data. You can disable iCloud for the App at any time in your device's Settings, and the App will continue to function using locally stored data only.

3. Data Collection

Fuelled UK does not collect any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not create or require a user account
  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Does not transmit your vehicle or fuel data to any external servers
  • Does not include analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks
  • Does not track your behaviour within the App or across other apps

4. In-App Purchase

Fuelled UK offers a one-time in-app purchase for lifetime access, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details. All transaction data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion

You can edit or delete individual fill-up records within the App at any time. Deleting the App from your device will remove all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can remove the App's iCloud data from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Fuelled UK does not knowingly collect any personal data from users of any age. As the App collects no personal data whatsoever, it is suitable for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the App after any changes are posted means you accept the updated Policy.
