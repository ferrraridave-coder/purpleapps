1. Description of the App

Fuelled UK is a fuel and MPG tracker for UK drivers. It allows you to record fill-ups, track fuel economy over time, and visualise running costs across one or more vehicles. Key features include:

  • Multi-vehicle support with colour-coded charts
  • Accurate UK MPG calculations using imperial gallons (4.546 litres)
  • Fill-up logging with litres, mileage, and total cost
  • Fill-up history with edit and delete support
  • MPG and cost per litre charts with trend lines
  • Automatic iCloud sync across devices

2. Accuracy and Limitations
