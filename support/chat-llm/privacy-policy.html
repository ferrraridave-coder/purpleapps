Privacy Policy for Chat LLM

Last updated: 20 January 2026

Chat LLM is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how Chat LLM handles your data and what information may be transmitted to external services depending on which features you use.

Useful links:
- Support Page

1. Built-In Local AI (Optional)

Chat LLM may include an optional on-device (local) AI model that runs entirely on your device. When using the local model:

  • No internet connection is required
  • No data is transmitted to any external servers
  • Conversations remain on your device only
  • No account or API key is required

We do not have access to any conversations generated by the local model.

2. Free Cloud Chat

Chat LLM provides a free cloud-based chat option that does not require an API key. When using free cloud chat:

  • Your text prompts are sent to our backend server
  • Our server forwards requests to the AI provider (currently Groq)
  • Responses are returned to your device
  • No user account is required

We do not store your prompts or responses. Messages are processed in real time and discarded after delivery. Free cloud chat is text-only and does not support image or document uploads.

3. Bring Your Own API Key (Optional)

Chat LLM allows you to optionally connect your own API keys to access additional AI models from third-party providers, such as:

  • OpenRouter
  • OpenAI
  • xAI (for voice chat)

When using your own API keys, you may input or upload:

  • Text prompts
  • Images (vision-capable models only)
  • Documents (PDF, Word, and text files, where supported)
  • Voice input (xAI voice chat only)

This content is transmitted directly from your device to the selected provider. Chat LLM does not store your prompts, images, documents, or API keys on its servers. API keys are stored securely on your device using iOS Keychain.

4. Voice Chat

Chat LLM supports optional real-time voice conversations powered by third-party services (such as xAI). Voice chat requires you to provide your own compatible API key.

  • Voice input is transmitted directly to the voice provider
  • Audio is processed in real time
  • Conversation transcripts are stored locally on your device

We do not store voice data or transcripts on our servers. You are responsible for any usage costs charged by the provider.

5. What Chat LLM Does Not Do

Chat LLM does not:

  • Store your prompts, uploads, or conversations on our servers
  • Store your API keys on our servers
  • Create or manage user accounts
  • Collect analytics or usage tracking data
  • Include advertising or marketing trackers
  • Sell or share data with advertisers

6. Third-Party Data Retention

While Chat LLM does not retain your data, third-party providers may log or store data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/
  • xAI: https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy/

These policies apply only when you choose to use those providers. They do not apply when using an on-device local model.

7. Security

All network communication is transmitted over HTTPS using industry-standard encryption. However, no internet transmission is entirely risk-free. You should avoid sending highly sensitive or confidential information through cloud-based AI services.

8. Your Rights

Because Chat LLM does not store personal data on our servers, there is no user data held by us to access, export, or delete. Any data retained by third-party providers is subject to their own policies and must be managed through them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Chat LLM after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
