Privacy Policy for Copy Tiles: Keyboard

Last updated: April 2026

Copy Tiles: Keyboard ("the App") is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data when you use it on iPhone or iPad.

Support Page Terms of Use Back to Copy Tiles

1. Data You Create

Copy Tiles lets you create and store text tiles and categories. This content is created entirely by you and stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. We do not have access to the content of your tiles, categories, or any text you save within the App.

2. iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled for the App, your tiles and categories will sync across all devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. We do not operate any servers that store or process your tile data. You can disable iCloud sync for the App at any time in your device's Settings.

3. Clipboard History

Copy Tiles can display your clipboard history within the keyboard for convenience. Clipboard history is stored locally on each device and is never synced to iCloud or transmitted to any external service. It remains private to the device you are using.

4. Keyboard Full Access

To enable iCloud sync and real-time updates between the main app and the keyboard extension, Copy Tiles requests Full Access for the keyboard. Apple requires this permission for any custom keyboard that needs to communicate outside its extension sandbox.

Copy Tiles uses this access solely to sync tiles via iCloud and to read clipboard content for the clipboard history feature. We do not use Full Access to log, transmit, or store your keystrokes or any other text you type. No text typed using the Copy Tiles keyboard is ever sent to us or any third party.

5. Data Collection

Copy Tiles does not collect any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not create or require a user account
  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Does not transmit tile content, clipboard data, or keystrokes to external servers
  • Does not include analytics SDKs or advertising networks
  • Does not track your behaviour within the App or across other apps

6. In-App Purchase

Copy Tiles offers a one-time in-app purchase for lifetime access, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details. All transaction data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete individual tiles and categories within the App at any time. To remove all data associated with the App from iCloud, you can delete the App from your device, which will prompt iOS to remove its associated iCloud data.

8. Children's Privacy

Copy Tiles does not knowingly collect any personal data from users of any age. As the App collects no personal data whatsoever, it is suitable for use by all ages.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the App after any changes are posted means you accept the updated Policy.
