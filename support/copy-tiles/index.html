Frequently Asked Questions

What is Copy Tiles: Keyboard?

Copy Tiles is a custom keyboard app for iPhone and iPad. You create a personal library of reusable text tiles — things like email addresses, prompts, replies, links, or any text you type often — and then insert them instantly from a custom keyboard in any app. No switching apps, no retyping.

How do I enable the Copy Tiles keyboard?

Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and select Copy Tiles. Once added, you can switch to it while typing by tapping the globe icon on your keyboard. For full functionality, tap “Allow Full Access” when prompted — this is required for iCloud sync and clipboard history to work.

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

Apple requires custom keyboards to have Full Access enabled in order to communicate with the main app and access iCloud. Copy Tiles uses this to sync your tiles across devices and to provide clipboard history. Copy Tiles does not send any of your data to external servers — everything stays within your iCloud account and on your device.

How do I create and organise tiles?

Open the Copy Tiles app and tap the + button to create a new tile. Give it a name and enter the text you want it to insert. You can assign tiles to categories and reorder both tiles and categories using drag-and-drop. Tile sizes can be adjusted in Settings — choose Small (4 per row), Medium (3 per row), or Large (2 per row) depending on how many tiles you want visible at once.

My tiles aren't showing up in the keyboard — what do I do?

First, make sure Full Access is enabled for the Copy Tiles keyboard in Settings. If it is, try closing and reopening the app to trigger a sync, then switch back to the keyboard. Real-time updates between the app and keyboard require Full Access to be turned on.

Does Copy Tiles sync across my devices?

Yes. Copy Tiles uses iCloud to sync your tiles and categories across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID. Changes made on one device will appear on others automatically. Note that clipboard history is device-local and does not sync across devices.

Does Copy Tiles collect any of my data?

No. Copy Tiles does not have any external servers, does not collect personal data, and does not include analytics or advertising. Your tiles are stored in your personal iCloud account. The content of your tiles and clipboard is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Is there a subscription?

No. Copy Tiles offers a full 7-day free trial. After the trial, a single one-time purchase unlocks lifetime access. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges. All payments are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the restore option in the app's settings or on the paywall screen. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at no cost on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

What devices does Copy Tiles support?

Copy Tiles requires an iPhone or iPad running iOS 18.6 or later.
