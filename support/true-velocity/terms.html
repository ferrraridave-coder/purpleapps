1. Description of the App

True Velocity is a GPS speedometer for iPhone. It uses your device's GPS and Core Location framework to calculate and display your real-time speed on a physically-tuned analogue dial with an accompanying digital readout. Key features include:

  • Real-time GPS speed tracking with adaptive smoothing
  • Physically-tuned analogue needle with natural inertia
  • Digital speed display alongside the analogue dial
  • Clean, distraction-free interface with no account required

2. GPS Accuracy and Limitations

True Velocity relies on GPS data provided by your iPhone. GPS accuracy can vary depending on environmental conditions, including but not limited to: signal obstruction from buildings, tunnels, or dense foliage; atmospheric conditions; and device hardware limitations. As a result:

  • Speed readings may not be perfectly accurate at all times
  • There may be a slight delay between your actual speed and the displayed speed
  • GPS signal may be temporarily lost in certain environments

True Velocity is not intended or suitable for use in speed-enforcement contexts, official timing, competitive motorsport, or any situation where precise speed measurement is legally or safety-critical.
