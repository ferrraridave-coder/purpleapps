Privacy Policy for True Velocity

Last updated: April 2026

True Velocity ("the App") is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data when you use it on iPhone.

Support Page Terms of Use Back to True Velocity

1. Location and GPS Data

True Velocity uses your device's GPS and Core Location framework to calculate your current speed in real time. When you use the live speedometer only, speed and location readings are calculated and displayed on your device.

If you enable trip recording, the App may record trip information such as route/location points, distance, duration, and speed-related journey data so the trip can be shown later in your trip history. This trip data is stored on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Data Collection

True Velocity does not collect any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not create or require a user account
  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Does not transmit speed history, routes, or travel patterns to external servers
  • Does not transmit any data to external servers

3. Location Permissions

True Velocity may request location access while using the app for live speed display. If you enable trip recording and want a trip to continue while the app is in the background, such as during a phone call, the App may request Always location access.

Always location access is used only to support background trip recording. You can change or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. On-Device Processing

All speed calculations and display logic run entirely on your device. There is no backend service, no cloud processing, and no network communication involved in the core functionality of the App. True Velocity does not require an internet connection to operate.

5. Analytics and Tracking

True Velocity does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks. We do not track your behaviour within the App, and we do not build any profile of you as a user.

6. In-App Purchase

True Velocity offers a one-time in-app purchase for lifetime access, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details. All transaction data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

True Velocity does not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the App after any changes are posted means you accept the updated Policy.
