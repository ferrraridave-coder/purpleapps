Frequently Asked Questions

What is True Velocity?

True Velocity is a GPS speedometer for iPhone. It tracks your real-time speed using your device's GPS and displays it on a physically-tuned analogue dial alongside a digital readout. The needle is designed to behave like a real vehicle speedometer — responding with natural inertia rather than jumping around with raw GPS fluctuations.

How accurate is the speed reading?

True Velocity uses the GPS signal provided by your iPhone to calculate speed. Accuracy depends on GPS signal quality, which can be affected by your environment (tall buildings, tunnels, dense tree cover, and so on). True Velocity is not intended for use in speed-enforcement contexts or competitive timing.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. True Velocity works entirely offline. It only requires a GPS signal from your device — no internet connection is needed and no data is ever sent off your device.

Does True Velocity collect my data?

No. True Velocity does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. GPS is used solely to calculate your current speed in real time and is never recorded or sent anywhere. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, and no account required.

Is there a subscription?

No subscriptions. True Velocity offers a full 7-day free trial. After the trial period, a single one-time purchase unlocks lifetime access to the app. All payments are handled securely by Apple through the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've already purchased True Velocity and need to restore it on a new device, tap the restore option in the app's settings or paywall screen. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at no extra cost on any device using the same Apple ID.

What devices does True Velocity support?

True Velocity requires an iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. The app uses your device's built-in GPS, so a GPS-equipped iPhone is required — it will not function accurately without a GPS signal.

Why does using GPS drain my battery?

Continued GPS use is one of the more power-intensive tasks an iPhone can perform. True Velocity uses GPS while the app is active, which may reduce battery life during extended use. We recommend keeping your device charged or connected to power when using True Velocity on long journeys.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please email a description of the issue or your feature idea, and include your device model and iOS version if relevant.
