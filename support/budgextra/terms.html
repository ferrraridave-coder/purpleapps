1. Description of the App

Budgextra is a free personal budgeting app for iPhone and iPad. It helps you track monthly bills, income, and one-off expenses, providing a clear overview of your financial position. Key features include:

  • Monthly bill tracking with due date management
  • Multi-source income tracking
  • One-time expense recording
  • Single-tap bill payment marking
  • Automatic month-to-month balance carryover
  • Optional overdraft support with customisable limits
  • Manual balance adjustment
  • Optional Face ID protection
  • Full offline functionality with optional iCloud sync
