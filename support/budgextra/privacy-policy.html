Privacy Policy for Budgextra

Last updated: April 2026

Budgextra ("the App") is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data when you use it on iPhone or iPad.

Support Page Terms of Use Back to Budgextra

1. Financial Data You Enter

Budgextra stores the financial information you enter — bills, income sources, expenses, and balance adjustments — locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable iCloud sync, in which case it is stored in your private Apple iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. We do not have access to any of your financial data at any time.

2. iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is entirely optional. If enabled, your budget data is synchronised across your devices using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us. You can disable iCloud sync for the App at any time in your device's Settings, and the App will continue to function fully offline.

3. Data Collection

Budgextra does not collect any personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not create or require a user account
  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Does not transmit your financial data to any external servers
  • Does not include analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks
  • Does not track your behaviour within the App or across other apps

4. Face ID

If you enable Face ID protection, the App uses Apple's Local Authentication framework to verify your identity on your device. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never accessible to the App or to us. We do not store or transmit any biometric information.

5. No Advertising

Budgextra is completely free and contains no advertising of any kind. There are no ad networks, no sponsored content, and no monetisation of your data. Your financial information is not used for any commercial purpose.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete individual bills, income entries, and expenses within the App at any time. Deleting the App from your device will remove all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can also remove the App's iCloud data from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Budgextra does not knowingly collect any personal data from users of any age. As the App collects no personal data whatsoever, it is suitable for all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the App after any changes are posted means you accept the updated Policy.
