Frequently Asked Questions

What is Budgextra?

Budgextra is a monthly budgeting app for iPhone and iPad. It lets you track your bills, income sources, and one-off expenses in one place, giving you a clear picture of where your money stands at any point in the month. Mark bills as paid with a single tap, and let Budgextra handle the month-to-month rollover automatically.

Is Budgextra really free?

Yes, completely. Budgextra is free to download and free to use with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions. There are no hidden costs or premium tiers. It is built to be genuinely useful for anyone trying to manage their finances, without charging them for the privilege.

How do I set up my bills and income?

Tap the + button on the main screen to add a bill, income source, or one-off expense. For bills, you can set the amount, due date, and whether they repeat monthly. Income sources work the same way — add as many as you have. Everything appears in your monthly overview automatically.

How does month-to-month carryover work?

At the start of each new month, Budgextra carries your remaining balance forward automatically. Any unpaid bills reset for the new month. You don't need to rebuild your budget from scratch — your recurring bills and income are already there waiting.

What is overdraft support?

If you have an overdraft facility on your bank account, you can enable overdraft support in Budgextra's settings and enter your overdraft limit. This extends your available balance by that amount. Budgextra will use visual indicators to clearly show when your balance is in the negative so you always know where you stand.

How do I adjust my balance manually?

Life doesn't always match the plan. If your actual bank balance differs from what Budgextra is showing, you can use the manual balance adjustment to bring them in line. Tap the balance figure on the main screen to find this option.

How do I enable Face ID?

Go to Settings within Budgextra and toggle on Face ID protection. Once enabled, the app will require Face ID (or your device passcode as a fallback) whenever you open it or return to it from the background.

Does Budgextra sync across my devices?

Yes, optionally. Budgextra supports iCloud sync, which keeps your budget data up to date across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID. You can disable iCloud sync entirely if you prefer to keep everything local to one device — the app works fully offline either way.

Does Budgextra collect any of my data?

No. Budgextra does not collect any personal data. All your financial information is stored locally on your device or in your private iCloud account. There are no external servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no account required.

What devices does Budgextra support?

Budgextra requires an iPhone or iPad running iOS 18.6 or later.
