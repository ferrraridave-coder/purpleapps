Privacy Policy for LLM Pro

Last updated: 20 January 2026

LLM Pro is developed and maintained by David Picknell ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how LLM Pro handles your data and what information may be transmitted to third-party services depending on how you use the app.

Useful links:
- Support Page
- Terms of Use

1. Chat and Image Content

When you use LLM Pro, you may input or upload:

  • Text prompts
  • Images (for image generation or image analysis)
  • Documents (PDF, Word, and text files, where supported)

This content is transmitted to third-party AI providers only to generate responses or images. LLM Pro does not store your prompts, images, documents, or responses on its own servers.

2. Premium Subscription

LLM Pro includes a paid subscription managed through Apple’s App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

3. AI Image Generation

Image generation and image editing features are powered by third-party AI services (including Replicate).

  • Images are generated remotely by the provider
  • No generated images are stored on our servers
  • Monthly image usage limits apply and are shown in-app

4. Bring Your Own API Key (Optional)

LLM Pro supports optional bring-your-own API keys for third-party providers such as OpenRouter, OpenAI, and xAI.

When using BYOK features:

  • Your content is sent directly to the selected provider
  • Your API keys are stored only on your device
  • You are responsible for provider usage costs

5. What LLM Pro Does Not Do

  • Store user prompts, images, or documents on our servers
  • Store API keys on our servers
  • Create or manage user accounts
  • Collect analytics or tracking data
  • Sell or share data with advertisers

6. Third-Party Providers

Depending on features used, LLM Pro may interact with:

  • Groq (cloud AI)
  • Replicate (image generation)
  • OpenRouter
  • OpenAI
  • xAI

These providers may retain data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Groq
  • Replicate
  • OpenRouter
  • OpenAI
  • xAI

7. Security

All network communication uses HTTPS encryption. You should avoid submitting highly sensitive or confidential information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of LLM Pro indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
